## About Me

Academic master’s student in Computer Science. I received my bachelor’s degree in Cybersecurity from Jinan University. I am currently pursuing my research at the ISEE Lab, Sun Yat‑sen University, under the supervision of Professor Weishi Zheng.

A former CTF‑Web competitor and ex‑member of Jinan University CTF team Xp0int. My research interests lie in spatial intelligence, multi‑agent systems and embodied intelligence. I am passionate about broad topics in artificial intelligence.

Outside of tech, I enjoy reading, music and video games. I also like observing and documenting daily life.

## Background

Jinan University, B.Eng. in Cybersecurity
Sun Yat‑sen University, M\.Sc\. in Computer Science (Academic Track), [Intelligence Science and System Lab (iSEE)](https://www.isee-ai.cn/index.html#/home), Supervisor: Professor Weishi Zheng

I have participated in CTF contests on the Web track, with solid knowledge of web vulnerabilities, penetration‑testing workflows and low‑level backend architectures.

## Let’s Connect!

Feel free to reach out for discussions on multi‑agent systems, spatial intelligence, embodied intelligence, general AI, cybersecurity and backend development. Technical exchanges and potential collaborations are both welcome.
